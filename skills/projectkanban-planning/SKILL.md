description: Clarify ProjectKanban features and turn decisions into specs and testable tickets.
---

Read the [design and planning guide](../../docs/design-and-planning.md).

1. Inspect code and existing terminology first. Ask one consequential unresolved question at a time.
2. Capture the goal, scope, acceptance scenarios, assumptions and decisions.
3. Split delivery into small end-to-end tickets with explicit dependencies and verification steps. For multi-session work, keep the next unresolved decision visible.
4. Keep plans local unless publishing is requested. Use fictional examples in public repo materials.
