---
name: projectkanban-progress
description: Keep ProjectKanban's development cards current as work starts, enters review, passes verification, or becomes blocked.
---

1. Identify the card and confirm its acceptance criteria before editing. Add a card for substantial work that has no matching scope.
2. Move planned work to Ready once prerequisites and planning are satisfied. Move it to In Progress when implementation begins. Record blockers and the next action on the card.
3. Move implemented work to Review with changed paths and checks performed. Move it to Done only after the card's required checks pass. Record exact evidence and disclose any remaining live checks.
4. Update the repo roadmap and its standalone projection together. For a browser with saved edits, merge a status update only if the card still has its prior status. Preserve user edits and surface conflicts for review.
5. Re-open a card if new evidence invalidates its result. Report the current card status and next required check in the handoff.
