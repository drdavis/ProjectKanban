name: projectkanban-design-review
description: Review ProjectKanban interfaces for hierarchy, accessibility and responsive behavior.
---

Read the [design and planning guide](../../docs/design-and-planning.md).

1. Inspect desktop and narrow widths with fictional data, including long titles, empty boards, filters and open sheets.
2. Check keyboard focus, Escape and focus return, horizontal scrolling and the status control.
3. Check text contrast on the composited glass surface. Record observable defects with reproduction steps and distinguish build checks from browser checks.
